# TikTok batch 1 — the problem, on camera

Five clips about the pain, not the product. Each one dramatizes a moment the
audience has lived — freezing under a question, replaying a meeting at 2am —
and ends on the same card. Everything is generated in Flow: paste the prompt,
generate, add the text overlays, post.

**End card on every clip (last 2–3 seconds, white text on black):**

> There's an app that grills you before the meeting does.
> **Waitlist open — link in bio.**

Bio link: flintroom.app. Turn on TikTok's AI-generated label (Flow watermarks
with SynthID anyway). Post one per day, strongest first.

---

## Clip 1 — The question you didn't prep

**Hook (overlay, first second):** "you rehearsed the slides. not the questions."

**Flow prompt:**
> Cinematic handheld shot inside a modern glass boardroom. A woman in her 30s
> in business casual stands mid-presentation, confident, gesturing at a chart.
> A silver-haired investor across the table leans back and interrupts flatly:
> "Stop — walk me through how you got that number." Her smile drops. Tight
> push-in on her face as she blinks, opens her mouth, nothing comes out. The
> room goes silent except the air conditioning. Realistic, shallow depth of
> field, cold office light. 8 seconds.

**Caption:** Everyone preps the deck. Nobody preps the ambush. #pitch #founder #corporatelife #publicspeaking

## Clip 2 — The 2am replay

**Hook (overlay):** "why do the perfect answers only show up at 2am"

**Flow prompt:**
> A man lies awake in bed at night, staring at the ceiling, face lit faintly
> blue by a phone on the nightstand showing 2:14 AM. Muffled echo of a
> meeting plays like a memory: a stern voice asking "so what makes you
> different from the incumbents?" and his own voice stammering "we... I mean,
> there are a few things..." He winces and pulls the pillow over his face.
> Moody, realistic, quiet. 8 seconds.

**Caption:** The shower answer. The 2am answer. Never the in-the-room answer. #interviewtips #founderlife #anxiety

## Clip 3 — Two slides in

**Hook (overlay):** "you never make it past slide 2"

**Flow prompt:**
> Realistic conference room scene. A young man presents, clicker in hand,
> slide 2 of 40 glowing behind him. A bored executive at the far end of the
> table cuts him off without looking up from her phone: "Skip ahead. What's
> the margin?" He fumbles the clicker, papers slide off the table in slow
> motion. Colleagues exchange glances. Overhead fluorescent light, documentary
> style. 8 seconds.

**Caption:** Real rooms don't wait for your agenda slide. #corporate #presentations #productmanager #consulting

---

## Clip 4 — The mirror doesn't ask follow-ups

**Hook (overlay):** "your mirror never asks follow-up questions"

**Flow prompt:**
> A woman in a blazer rehearses a pitch to her bathroom mirror at night,
> notecards in hand, speaking with confidence: "...and that's why this is a
> billion-dollar opportunity." She smiles at her reflection, satisfied. Hard
> cut to the same line the next day in a fluorescent-lit boardroom — five
> unimpressed faces staring back in dead silence. Her confidence visibly
> drains. Realistic, warm bathroom light then cold office light. 8 seconds.

**Caption:** Practicing alone is rehearsing the easy version. #jobinterview #pitchpractice #salarynegotiation

## Clip 5 — Tomorrow, 9am

**Hook (overlay):** "you know the slides. you don't know the questions."

**Flow prompt:**
> Night before a big presentation. A man paces a dim living room in a
> t-shirt, laptop open on the coffee table showing a slide deck, mouthing
> words to himself, checking notes, shaking his head and starting over.
> Clock shows 11:47 PM. Rain on the window. He sits, stares at the deck,
> whispers "what are they going to ask me..." Cinematic, intimate, realistic.
> 8 seconds.

**Caption:** The deck is done. That was never the hard part. #presentation #founder #careertok
