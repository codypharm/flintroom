# The hero film — "Get grilled first"

One story, six Flow shots, ~45 seconds. The arc: fear → a friend's push →
practice with Flintroom → the real pitch → the same question → delivered.

## Ground rules

- **Same actor in every shot.** Use Flow's character consistency
  (ingredients): generate the founder once, reuse him. And repeat his
  description word-for-word in every prompt — it is written once below and
  pasted into each shot.
- **The app's screen is never generated.** Shoot the phone dark or softly
  glowing; composite the real Flintroom screenshot / screen recording over
  it in the edit. Flow-invented UI is the one thing viewers will freeze-frame.
- The friend placing the phone **flat on the desk** is the signature image —
  hands-free is the product. Give that beat room; don't rush the cut.
- Turn on TikTok's AI-generated label. End on the same card as every clip.

**THE FOUNDER (paste into every prompt):** "a man in his early 30s with
short dark hair and light stubble, wearing a navy t-shirt"

**THE QUESTION (the thread that ties shot 4 to shot 6):**
"Walk me through that margin number — your deck says something different."

---

## Shot 1 — The worry (8s)

> Evening living room, warm lamp light. THE FOUNDER sits hunched on a sofa,
> laptop open on the coffee table showing a slide deck, notecards scattered.
> He rubs his face, exhausted. His friend, a woman in her 30s in a hoodie,
> walks in with two mugs, stops, and asks: "Okay — what's going on with
> you?" Realistic, cinematic, shallow depth of field, intimate.

## Shot 2 — Naming the fear (8s)

> Same living room, same light. THE FOUNDER leans back and admits: "Pitch
> is Thursday. I know the deck cold... it's the questions. They're going to
> tear it apart and I'm going to just stand there." The friend sits on the
> sofa arm, listening, thoughtful. Realistic, intimate, close on his face.

## Shot 3 — The phone on the desk (8s) — THE SIGNATURE SHOT

> Same living room. The friend picks his phone up off the coffee table,
> places it flat and face-up on the desk next to the laptop — a deliberate,
> unhurried gesture, camera low at desk level — and says: "Then get grilled
> before they grill you. Practice with Flintroom." The phone screen glows
> softly. THE FOUNDER looks at it, skeptical but curious. Cinematic, the
> phone in sharp focus.
>
> EDIT: composite the real Flintroom screenshot onto the glowing screen.

## Shot 4 — The practice (8s)

> Same room, later — the friend is gone, one lamp on. THE FOUNDER stands
> and pitches to the phone lying flat on the desk, hands free, mid-flow. A
> sharp, skeptical voice from the phone interrupts him: "Walk me through
> that margin number — your deck says something different." He freezes,
> exhales, nods slowly like a boxer taking a hit, and starts his answer
> again. Determined. Cinematic, moody, realistic.

## Shot 5 — The real room (8s)

> Bright glass boardroom, daytime. THE FOUNDER, now in a blazer over the
> navy t-shirt, presents to three investors. A silver-haired investor leans
> forward and asks: "Walk me through that margin number — your deck says
> something different." Tight on THE FOUNDER's face: a flicker of
> recognition, then the smallest smile. Realistic, cinematic.

## Shot 6 — Delivery (8s)

> Same boardroom. THE FOUNDER answers with total calm, gesturing precisely
> at the chart behind him, unshaken, mid-sentence: "Glad you asked — the
> deck shows blended margin, here's the split..." The investors exchange an
> impressed glance; one nods and writes something down. Warm confident
> light. Slow push-in.

## End card (3s, white on black)

> He heard the question before they asked it.
> **Flintroom — waitlist open. Link in bio.**

## Edit notes

- The cut from shot 4 to shot 5 is the whole film: same question, two
  rooms. If timing allows, let the investor's first words start over the
  last frames of the practice shot (J-cut) so the two rooms blur together.
- Burned-in captions throughout — most viewers are muted.
- Post as the pinned video on the profile; the five pain clips drive
  traffic, this one closes.
