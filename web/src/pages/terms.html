What Flintroom is

Flintroom is a rehearsal room: AI panelists question you about your own argument and material, and an AI coach helps you rebuild the answers that broke. It is a practice tool. It is not financial, legal, investment, or professional advice of any kind. The panelists challenge you the way a hostile audience might, and like a hostile audience they can be mistaken, unfair, or simply wrong. Their arithmetic, their claims, and their opinions are generated by AI models and should never be relied on as fact. What they are for is making you sharper.

The panel is also confrontational by design. Being interrupted, pressed, and challenged is the product working, not the product misbehaving.
