Every other tool scores your delivery. This one attacks your argument.

Filler words, pace, eye contact. Fine things to polish, and none of them are why a pitch dies. A pitch dies when somebody unimpressed asks the question you hoped nobody would, and your answer comes apart in front of everyone.

Flintroom rehearses that moment. The substance, under fire, against your own material. So the hardest question you hear next week is one you have already answered twice.
